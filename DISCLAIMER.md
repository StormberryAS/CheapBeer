## Alcohol, health and the nature of this list

**This application does not promote, encourage or incentivise the consumption of
alcohol, and it does not endorse or recommend any establishment listed.** It is a
price list, nothing more. No establishment pays to appear, none is affiliated with
Stormberry AS, and their inclusion is neither a recommendation of the venue nor of
drinking there.

**Alcohol is harmful.** It is causally linked to liver disease, cardiovascular
disease, several cancers including breast, bowel, liver, mouth and throat, mental
illness and dependency. The World Health Organization states that **no level of
alcohol consumption is safe for health**, and Norwegian health authorities treat
alcohol as both harmful and addictive. Risk rises with quantity and there is no
threshold below which it disappears.

**Do not drink to excess. Never drink and drive, and never operate machinery,
vehicles or vessels after drinking.** Norway's legal limit is 0.2 per mille, among
the strictest in the world, and the safe amount before driving is none.

If alcohol is a problem for you or someone close to you, help exists:
**Rustelefonen 08588** (rustelefonen.no), your fastlege, or **AKAN** through your
employer.

**Nothing here may be relied upon by anyone under the legal drinking age**, which in
Norway is 18 for beer and wine and 20 for spirits.

**The prices are unverified and are not supplied by the establishments.** They are
submitted by members of the public and auto-approved once three submissions agree,
a mechanism that resists casual error and defeats nobody determined to enter
nonsense. Some entries may have been produced or completed with AI assistance.
Prices go stale, differ by time of day, by measure, by tap and by day of the week,
and may simply be wrong. Ask at the bar.

measurements, and they are not sourced from an official provider unless the

Astronomical and tidal figures are calculations from published algorithms and
carry the error of those algorithms and of the input data. Local conditions,
terrain, weather, atmospheric refraction, harbour geometry and human error can
